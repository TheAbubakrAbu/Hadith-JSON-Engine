# Changelog

All notable changes to the data and tooling. The corpus is 50,884 hadiths across 17 collections throughout; entries below describe what changed *about* those records.

## [1.2]

### Added

- **App catalog sync**: [`tools/sync_app_catalog.py`](tools/sync_app_catalog.py) pulls `db/catalog.json`'s prose back from the app that writes it. This is the one file here whose text flows INWARD. Everything else is upstream of the app: the corpus is repaired here and the packs are built from it, so a disagreement about hadith text means the app is stale. The catalog's titles, author names and descriptions are the opposite, display copy written for the consuming app's own shelf, which is exactly why `verify_packs.py` cross-checks them against `HadithCatalogBook.all`. The tool reuses that verifier's own Swift regexes on purpose, since a second parser could "fix" the gate into silence, and it refuses the whole run rather than carry a bad rewrite across: every Arabic change must leave the consonantal skeleton identical once the marks are stripped, and no incoming text may contain a sukoon. That is what separates a vocalization pass from a corrected name hiding inside one. [docs/01-data-schema.md](docs/01-data-schema.md#the-catalog--dbcatalogjson)
- **[NOTICE](NOTICE)**: what the MIT license does not cover. `LICENSE` used to carry a note about the hadith text appended after the MIT terms, and GitHub identifies a license by comparing the file against known texts and tolerates only small deviations, so the appendix made the repository read as "Other" instead of MIT. The terms are unchanged; only their location is. The hadeethenc.com conditions are stated there too, beside the sunnah.com ones.
- **[docs/whats-new.md](docs/whats-new.md)**: what each release added, newest first, at the level the GitHub releases describe rather than the corpus-by-corpus detail this file carries.

### Fixed

- **73 Perso-Arabic characters reached readers as blank circles.** 61 word forms across two books were typed on a keyboard producing the Persian letters where the Arabic ones belong: `ی` FARSI YEH for `ي` YEH or `ى` ALEF MAKSURA, `ک` KEHEH for `ك` KAF, `ۃ` TEH MARBUTA GOAL for `ة` TEH MARBUTA. These are ordinary Arabic words, not Persian ones, so the text reads the same and nothing looked wrong in the JSON. It mattered because the KFGQPC Uthmanic faces an app renders hadith in are Quran-only: all three codepoints sit in their cmap mapped onto the placeholder RING glyph, and because they are IN the cmap the OS never substitutes another face, so every one of them reached a reader as a small circle where a letter should be and no font fallback could rescue it. 68 of the 73 were in `shahwaliullah40`, across 28 of its 40 narrations, which is why that one collection read as broken while everything around it looked fine. The fold is per WORD and not per character, because Persian writes one `ی` for both `ي` and `ى` and a blind character map turns `عَلَی` into a misspelling of `عَلَى`; each of the 61 rows was settled twice, by corpus frequency and by an orthographic rule, and the two agreed on all 61. [`tools/fix_perso_arabic_letters.py`](tools/fix_perso_arabic_letters.py), whose `--verify` re-derives the table from the rest of the corpus.
- **`db/catalog.json`'s Arabic had drifted from the app's.** Al-Islam vocalized its authored Arabic (tashkeel on every letter except that a sukoon is never written) and this file was left behind: 36 fields across all 17 books, every `authorArabic` and every `longDescription`, whose Arabic is the author's name in parentheses, plus two `arabicTitle`s. Those 36 were the only failures `verify_packs.py` reported; all 666,816 text assertions passed throughout, so the corpus itself was never affected.

## [1.1] - 9 September 2026

The second corpus, and the search that reaches into it.

### Added

- **The Hadith Encyclopedia**: [`db/hadeethenc/`](db/hadeethenc): **2,328 narrations** under **452 categories**, each with a scholarly explanation, a benefits list, a grading, an attribution and a full takhrij reference, in Arabic and English, plus Arabic word glosses on 1,479 of them. This is the first corpus here that is **not** descended from sunnah.com: everything under `db/by_book/` traces back to one scrape of that site, and so do both donor projects used to repair and grade it, so a mistake upstream of all three was reachable through every path this engine offered and invisible to any cross-check between them. hadeethenc.com is an independent translation effort, and the only source carrying an explanation with each narration. Its terms permit reuse on two conditions, no modification of the content and a clear credit; [`tools/build_hadeethenc.py`](tools/build_hadeethenc.py) normalises structure only, its one text touch being CRLF to LF plus an outer trim. [docs/05-hadeethenc.md](docs/05-hadeethenc.md)
- **HENC format v2**: the encyclopedia's container, specified byte by byte: a 145 KB xz header holding the category tree and a light row per narration (everything a list, a category screen or a search needs), then 19 xz blocks of 128 full narrations that nothing reads until one is opened. 14.8 MB of JSON becomes **2.9 MB**, and browsing the whole encyclopedia touches no block at all. Packer [`tools/pack/pack_hadeethenc.py`](tools/pack/pack_hadeethenc.py), reference decoder [`tools/read_henc.py`](tools/read_henc.py) written from the spec rather than from the packer. [docs/05-hadeethenc.md](docs/05-hadeethenc.md#the-henc-container)
- **Ranked search, portable**: [`tools/ranked_search.py`](tools/ranked_search.py) scores a hadith by **where** the query's words landed (chapter title 12, collection 7, body 3) instead of scanning for the query as one contiguous run, with a phrase bonus, a whole-word bonus, a length-guarded stem, the transatlantic spelling at full price, and a corrected typo at a discount. Measured against the byte scan over all 50,884 records: `controlling anger` 0 hits becomes 4, `rights of the neighbour` 0 becomes 47, `intetion` 0 becomes 333. The corpus itself mixes the spellings (125 records say `neighbour`, 138 say `neighbor`), which is why that rule is not a guess. Standard library only; this and `fold.py` are the two files a port translates. [docs/06-ranked-search.md](docs/06-ranked-search.md)
- **Search vocabulary**: [`db/vocabulary.txt`](db/vocabulary.txt): **32,555 words**, every 4 to 24 letter lowercase-ASCII run in the English search fold of every hadith. Typo correction needs the corpus's own words, not a dictionary: a general word list corrects a narrator's name into an ordinary word and offers corrections toward words the corpus does not contain. Derived by [`tools/build_vocabulary.py`](tools/build_vocabulary.py) through the same two transforms the packer applies, and cross-checked against Al-Islam's copy, which that app exports from its own walk over the built `.hpk` packs: **two independent derivations, one from the JSON and one from the binaries, agreeing word for word.**
- **Subject index**: [`db/topics.json`](db/topics.json): **331 narrations** under 21 subjects in 7 lanes, curated by Jamil Hammoudeh (Tilawa, used with permission). Chapter browsing serves the corpus; this serves the question, and 14 of the 21 subjects draw on more than one collection. **No text is copied**: every entry is a `slug` + `citation` pair resolved against `db/by_book/`, so a curated row can never drift from the corpus behind it and a repair made later reaches every row for free. A citation not on the shelf fails the build. [docs/07-topics.md](docs/07-topics.md)
- **Meaning search**: [docs/08-semantic-search.md](docs/08-semantic-search.md) and [`tools/semantic.py`](tools/semantic.py): word-vector MaxSim over the corpus, with the `.svec` vector pack specified byte by byte. Ranked search cannot answer a question phrased in none of the corpus's words, and that is a real ceiling, not a tuning problem. Scoring a narration by a sentence embedding ranks this corpus close to randomly (hadith are dense; one vector per narration washes out the idea the query asks about), so a text is scored as the mean over the query's words of the best matching word in it, with a floor of `max(0.38, 0.85 x best)` so a query with no real answer returns nothing instead of its least bad guesses. **No model and no vectors ship**: the embedder is the consumer's, and both `.svec` guards (the model revision and a fingerprint of the source texts) exist so a stale pack is rejected rather than trusted.
- **Corpora gate**: [`tools/verify_corpora.py`](tools/verify_corpora.py): the acceptance test for everything in `db/` that is not `db/by_book/`, standard library only, **39,685 assertions**. It re-derives the vocabulary from the corpus and compares it word for word, re-runs every ranked-search probe, resolves every one of the 331 curated citations against the books, walks the category tree for orphans and cycles, and with `--pack` decodes a `.henc` and compares it to `db/hadeethenc/` field by field. `conformance/vectors.json` grew five sections for it and is now version 2. `--softened-dashes` relaxes the commentary comparison for a pack whose English text was re-punctuated downstream, holding it to structure and wording instead of characters: the same paragraph blocks, every dash-free block character for character, the same words in the same order in the rest, and no em dash left behind. That mode is what caught Al-Islam's dash filter rejoining its sentence split with a single space and flattening **86 paragraph breaks across 12 narrations**, turning long explanations into one wall of text. Fixed upstream in the app; the check stays.

## [1.0.0] - 10 August 2026

The repair, the data, and the pack format.

### Added

- **Pack verification gate**: [`tools/verify_packs.py`](tools/verify_packs.py) re-derives every string, id, citation, chapter range, daily flag, and search fold from `db/by_book` and compares it against the shipped packs, then asserts every section of `conformance/vectors.json` and the manifest checksums: **666,816 assertions** on an LZFSE build, 768,584 when the search payload is LZMA (`HPK_SEARCH=2`). Standard library only, so a port can use it as its own acceptance test. `tools/pack/build.sh` now runs it and fails the build on any mismatch. Packing successfully was never the same as packing *correctly*: a stale rebuild, an edited JSON that was never repacked, or a packer bug all produce files that decode perfectly and ship the wrong text; nothing caught those before.
- **The search fold, portable**: [`tools/fold.py`](tools/fold.py) is `HadithFold.swift` with no Apple frameworks and nothing outside the standard library: the Swift original leans on `CharacterSet`, this one spells out the Unicode categories it means. It reproduces the packer's fold fingerprint (`87ae980f6662910b`) and, verified across the whole corpus, every one of the 101,768 per-row search folds character for character. This is the file to translate when porting the engine: the fold is the one part a port can get wrong *silently*, since a mismatched fold returns no results rather than an error.
- **Collection catalog**: [`db/catalog.json`](db/catalog.json): the 17 collections as data rather than as someone's UI code, titles in both scripts (Arabic vocalized), compiler and era, short and long descriptions, and the 102 name aliases needed to resolve a reference like `"nasa'i 5"`. Held apart from `db/by_book/` so the book files stay pure upstream schema, and cross-checked against the corpus, and against the consuming app's compiled copy, by the verifier, so the two can no longer drift. [docs/01-data-schema.md](docs/01-data-schema.md#the-catalog--dbcatalogjson)
- **Standard citation numbers**: `citation` on **47,476 records (93.3%)**: the sunnah.com number readers actually cite ("2950", "8a"), which upstream's `idInBook` drifts from progressively (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2950 sat at `idInBook` 3033: a user report). Content-matched from two independent donors with a structural guard against anthology-duplicate mispairs; cross-confirmed on 24,479 records with 100% final agreement, after adjudicating seven scrape-era sunnah.com typos by local sequence (all seven confirmed by the second donor). Absence means *no standard number exists* (all of Muwatta Malik, most of Bulugh al-Maram, sunnah.com numbers neither), never "unknown". [`tools/add_citations.py`](tools/add_citations.py) · [docs/01-data-schema.md](docs/01-data-schema.md#citation)
- **HPK format v3**: the per-row record grew from 15 to 20 bytes to carry the citation (u32 base + u8 suffix). Spec, reference decoder, and conformance vectors updated; v2 readers reject v3 packs by design. [docs/04-hpk-format.md](docs/04-hpk-format.md)
- **HPK format v4**: the display payload grew from three to four strings per hadith: the scholar gradings (`name U+001F grade` records joined by `U+001E`), so apps can finally show sahih/hasan/da'if without carrying the JSON. The gradings had been in `db/by_book/` since `add_grades.py` ran; the packs simply dropped them. +0.2 MB across all 17 packs. [docs/04-hpk-format.md](docs/04-hpk-format.md)
- **Scholar gradings**: `english.grades` on **22,764 records (44.7%)**, from 10+ named scholars (Zubair Ali Zai, Al-Albani, Darussalam, Shuaib Al Arnaut, and others). Matched by content, never by hadith number. Nothing computed or adjudicated; where scholars differ, every verdict is kept. [`tools/add_grades.py`](tools/add_grades.py) · [docs/03-gradings.md](docs/03-gradings.md)
- **Documentation suite**: `docs/` with getting-started, architecture, data schema, repair pipeline, gradings, the byte-level pack format, porting guide, glossary, and FAQ.
- **Portable pack specification**: [docs/04-hpk-format.md](docs/04-hpk-format.md) documents `.hpk` completely, so the format is no longer readable only from the Swift source.
- **Reference pack decoder**: [`tools/read_pack.py`](tools/read_pack.py), ~200 lines of standard-library Python written from the spec alone. Verifies all 7,277 Bukhari rows.
- **Conformance vectors**: [`conformance/vectors.json`](conformance/vectors.json), behavioural truth any port can assert against.
- **Data dictionary**: [`db/README.md`](db/README.md).

### Fixed

- **[docs/PORTING.md](docs/PORTING.md) claimed `read_pack.py --verify` asserts the conformance vectors.** It does not, and never did: it is a decode check that prints per-book counts. A port following that instruction would have believed it had passed a conformance suite it never ran. The two tools and what each actually proves are now spelled out.
- **290 further hadiths repaired** by a second, line-aware pass. Pass 1's simulation used a single greedy strip over the whole string, but the upstream regex is per-line and both donors ship the text flattened, so any hadith with a bracketed insertion *and* a bracketed citation could never satisfy the proof. Those records were **unprovable, not undamaged**. [`tools/repair_line_aware.py`](tools/repair_line_aware.py) · [docs/02-repair-pipeline.md](docs/02-repair-pipeline.md)
  - Nawawi's Forty went from 19 damaged records to **zero**, including #3 (`[pillars]`) and #9 (`[to do]`, an entire missing clause).
  - 174 of the 290 are confirmed by two independent donors; 116 rest on one.
- **646 pass-1 repairs opened with a narrator fragment**: `"): Two men set out…"`. The proof compares with `norm`, which discards punctuation, so a split before the `):` closing a narrator and one after it were indistinguishable to it. Proof-neutral to fix. [`tools/fix_leading_punctuation.py`](tools/fix_leading_punctuation.py)
- **Donor loading silently failed on most books.** The CheeseWithSauce files carry a UTF-8 BOM; `json.load` threw and the error was swallowed, leaving books with one donor instead of two. Now read as `utf-8-sig`, and unreadable files are reported rather than skipped.

### Changed

- README restructured around what the engine *is* and what is still wrong with it.
- **Corrected the residual-damage figure.** The previous "~2,750 further records carry the signature of a deletion" reads as a damage count and is not one. Measured against the nearest clean donor: **2,562 lost nothing** (the scar regex over-fires ~130:1), **19 are genuinely damaged**, **22 undecidable**. The honest residual is 19 + 22, not ~2,750.

### Known issues

- 19 records still provably truncated; 22 undecidable; 16 Ibn Majah records refused as ambiguous. All need a third source.
- 115 repairs rest on a single donor (`confirmed_by: 1` in the logs).
- 1,252 gradings refused for contradictory attribution to the same scholar.
- Darimi has no English translation at all (3,406 records); sunnah.com has none either.
- Missing hadiths and chapter gaps are inherited from upstream. `idInBook` drift is answered by the `citation` field, but the row index itself still drifts and remains a row key, not a citation.

- **4,187 hadiths repaired** from the greedy-bracket bug in `AhmedBaset/hadith-json`, using two independent clean scrapes and a proof gate: re-simulating the bug on a candidate must reproduce the damaged record exactly, so a mis-attributed repair is structurally impossible. Matching by content, never by hadith number. [`tools/final_repair.py`](tools/final_repair.py)
  - Reported upstream as [issue #17](https://github.com/AhmedBaset/hadith-json/issues/17).
  - Worked example: Forty Hadith Qudsi 24, where the deletion inverted the meaning so the hadith read *"If Allah has loved a servant … I abhor So-and-so"*.
- **Full audit trail**: `logs/<book>.repairlog.json` holds `before`/`after` for every change.
- **The `.hpk` pack format and packer**: 79 MB of JSON to 25 MB, memory-mapped, block-lazy, with whitespace cleanup, search folds, chapter row ranges, and daily-card flags all precomputed at build time. Fold and blocked-word fingerprints stamped into every pack so the two repositories cannot silently drift. [`tools/pack/`](tools/pack)
- Honorific rendering normalised to the `ﷺ` ligature (4,148 occurrences).
